## What to look for
- Resolution ≥ 2000 px on the long side; no heavy compression, no screenshots with UI.
- Clear, unobstructed face, good even light, no sunglasses/hats if possible.
- Three kinds, ideally: **Kada alone**, **Miloud alone**, **both together** (waist-up or full body), plus a shot holding a Hipz bottle.
- Plain or simple backgrounds are easiest to cut out; a clean, bright wall/studio is best. Prefer images from their own channels or a shoot you own the rights to.
- Landscape and portrait versions (hero is landscape on desktop, portrait on mobile).

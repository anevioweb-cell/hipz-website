## 3. Brand voice — **GUESS, not evidence**

Inferred only from the fact that it is a creator-led drink for a young Algerian audience:
- Playful, quick, a bit cheeky; short punchy lines; humour that echoes the duo's comedic style.
- Mix of Algerian Arabic flavour and English/French; Arabic accent words (e.g. a greeting or a hype word) used sparingly.
- Sample *placeholder* direction only: "Pop the can. Pass it on." — **an example of tone, not a proposed slogan; no tagline is known.**

## 4. Audience (assumption)

Young Algerian consumers (teens to 30s) who follow Kada & Miloud on YouTube/Instagram/TikTok; secondary audience: retailers/distributors (the brief asks for a /distributors page). Unverified.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. Exact relationship of Kada & Miloud to Hipz, and have they approved this site/pitch?
2. Official logo (vector), can designs, colours, fonts, tagline.
3. Confirmed flavours (lemon + peach only? more?) with real names, Arabic names, can sizes.
4. Which product claims are allowed (ingredients, sugar, calories)? Anything regulated in Algeria to avoid?
5. Which wilayas / cities are distributed in; who are the distributors?
6. Which contact details may be public (the two phone numbers? an email?)
7. Is "Hayboz" a character, campaign or typo?
8. Creator photos/videos: do they supply them and with what permission?
9. Site language: English + small Arabic accents (as proposed) or fuller Arabic/French?
10. Is there a company legal name, address, and a manufacturer for the footer?
